The first figure highlights the largest and deadliest conflicts recorded between 1989 and 2023, categorized by conflict type:

  • State-based conflicts (black) involve governments and organized armed groups.
  • Non-state conflicts (orange) occur between non-governmental groups.
  • One-sided violence (red) represents deliberate attacks on civilians.
