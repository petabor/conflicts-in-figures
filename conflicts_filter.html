This section allows users to explore the 150 most devastating conflicts recorded between 1989 and 2023. Each conflict can be selected through a filter, displaying two complementary figures below.

The first figure provides an overview of the conflict, showing the two main opposing sides (A and B, where A is always the aggressor in one-sided type of conflict), the distribution of fatalities, and the share of civilian deaths. The accompanying pie chart summarizes the proportions of deaths attributed to each side and to civilians. If side B represents civilians, it is displayed only in the pie chart and not as a bar.

{% include panel.html layout="col-center" cells=page.top_img %}

The second figure presents a random sample of news headlines related to the selected conflict, drawn from articles associated with the underlying dataset.

If no headlines are shown, it does not necessarily mean that no reports exist; some may have been excluded during data cleaning. Every fatality record in the dataset is linked to a specific report.
