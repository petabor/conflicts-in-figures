Welcome to this page exploring conflicts through numbers and figures.

These statistics are tragic, yet they are essential for understanding the scope of both past and ongoing events. The goal here is not to reduce human suffering to mere numbers, but to use data to convey the magnitude and gravity of these tragedies.

  

Most figures and graphs are based on datasets from the Uppsala Conflict Data Program (Department of Peace and Conflict Research, Uppsala University in Sweden).

  

In particular, the following dataset:

  • UCDP Georeferenced Event Dataset (GED)

  

Research papers for a reference:

  • Davies, S., Pettersson, T., Sollenberg, M., & Öberg, M. (2025). Organized violence 1989–2024, and the challenges of identifying civilian victims. Journal of Peace Research, 62(4).
  • Sundberg, Ralph and Erik Melander (2013) Introducing the UCDP Georeferenced Event Dataset. Journal of Peace Research 50(4).
  • UCDP is part of and funded by DEMSCORE, national research infrastructure grant 2021-00162 from the Swedish Research Council.
