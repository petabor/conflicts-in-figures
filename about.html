Welcome to this page exploring conflicts through numbers and figures.

These kinds of statistics are tragic, but they are necessary for understanding the scope of past and current events. These efforts are not meant to reduce suffering to a number, but rather to use numbers to convey the severity of these tragic events.

Creating this website was a personal project. It deepened my awareness of world history and helped me feel the human tragedy unfolding within this limited time window. It made me even more empathetic toward all people across the world, regardless of nationality, race, or social class.

I wish to carry some of this to you through this very depressing website. Whatever shortcomings this website has, please note that it was created with genuine intentions to learn and understand, and not to spread misinformation or moralize.
