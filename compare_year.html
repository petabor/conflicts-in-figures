This section lets you compare any two selected years from 1989 to 2023, and see how they differ in terms of conflict intensity and human cost. You can choose from four metrics listed in the drop-down menu below.

Once a metric is selected, the visualization displays the values for the two years side-by-side, and also lists the top five countries in each selected year that experienced the highest death tolls (for the fatalities metric) or greatest number of conflicts (for the frequency metric).

This feature helps highlight how conflict patterns shift over time — for example, how the scale of violence or number of incidents in a given year compares to another, and which countries were driving those peaks or changes.
